    Cloth first. Logo last.

    MEHAR (ਮਿਹਰ, grace) is a Punjabi house that treats heritage as a material, not a print. Phulkari geometry is thread-guided by hand, Gurmukhi is embossed rather than screened, and every piece is cut long and wide for a 6 ft frame.
