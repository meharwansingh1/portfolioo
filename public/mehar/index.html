            Size chart

            Every measurement,
            in the open.

            Garment measurements, laid flat. Chest is measured armpit to armpit and doubled.

            How to measure

            Three numbers,
            one tape.

            1. 01

              Chest

              Tape under the arms, around the fullest part of the chest, level all the way round. Breathe out, don't pull tight.

            2. 02

              Waist

              Where your trousers actually sit — usually just below the navel. Keep one finger behind the tape.

            3. 03

              Height

              Barefoot, back to a wall, turban off. Every MEHAR size is graded from a 6 ft (183 cm) fit model.
